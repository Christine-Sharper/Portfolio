REEL

Role:Team Lead
Timeline:August - November 2025
Tools:Figma, Figjam

Introduction

Film lovers don’t have to feel alone in their interests. REEL is a mobile prototype designed to connect movie and TV watchers through unprecedented features compared to its competitors. Leading a team of three through the class's Goal-Directed Design method, we: conducted user interviews, completed exercises in affinity mapping, created personas, built wireframes, and conducted usability tests, resulting in a full prototype.

My personal responsibilities as team lead consisted of task delegation; facilitating interviews and usability testing, managing consent forms, taking meeting notes; scheduling meeting times; handing in deliverables; and fostering team connection while keeping us focused on the end goal.

Problem

The current state of movie review platforms has focused primarily on ranking, rating, and leaving comments about media without emphasis on live interaction between people with similar media interests. What existing products/services fail to address is the need for connection through community engagement.

Solutions

The foundation: a social feed for discovering and sharing movie and TV ratings and reviews.

A section for users to discover media more easily through organized categories.

Media pages with clean UI and reccomendations.

Users have the ability to organize and revisit media using bookmarks and custom lists.

Direct messages and review replies enable users to connect and discuss shared interests in real time.

Lessons Learned

This project allowed me to reflect on my visual design consistency in practice. I learned that applying appropriate spacing, margins, and design patterns across the product is what takes something from a flat draft to a true prototype. I also learned the importance of usability testing and how user feedback can reveal issues that may not be obvious during the design process. As team lead, I developed stronger task delegation and collaboration skills. Effective communication and my consistent efforts towards team bonding contributed to a more productive workflow.

Feline / Whisker Wardrobe

Branding and Visual Design for Two Cat‑Focused Companies

Role:Solo Project
Timeline:1 week
Tools:Figma

Introduction

A brand’s personality directly influences how its visual identity comes together. I designed landing pages and accompanying social media posts for two fictional cat companies. Whisker Wardrobe is playful, bold, exciting, vibrant; FELINE is clean, modern, premium, sleek. My goal was to build two distinct visual systems that express each brand’s personality through color, typography, layout, and imagery.

Developing Brand Directions

I designed Whisker Wardrobe to have a few bright and energetic colors that I could use together to create an inviting and playful atmosphere for the brand. I chose a grounded lime green to represent loyalty, orange to represent enthusiasm and freedom (freedom of expression), and bright purple for art and the imagination put into the products’ designs. In contrast, I knew I wanted FELINE to be represented by a deep, navy-blue-leaning color, which would represent elevated and thoughtful clothing design. I chose a lighter, soft blue to compliment it. That, along with white, is used to highlight smaller but important details and represents peace (peace of mind, for the safety and comfort of customers’ cats).

Final Deliverables

The final product resulted in two carefully crafted brand experiences as seen in each of their landing pages and social media posts. I also designed my own vector icons for each brand.

Whisker Wardrobe is a cat clothing company specializing in colorful and whimsical hats. To reflect the playful nature of the products, I created a vibrant visual identity using bright colors and friendly typography while still maintaining clear visual hierarchy throughout the website and social media assets. Through asymmetrical compositions, varied shapes, and playful alignment choices, I aimed to create a brand experience that feels creative, energetic, and approachable.

FELINE is a cat apparel brand focused on comfortable, curated clothing collections. I developed a clean and minimalist visual identity by emphasizing symmetry, consistency, and clear visual hierarchy to create a balanced user experience. The resulting brand feels modern and organized while highlighting the comfort and quality of the products.
